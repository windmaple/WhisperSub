AI Video Subtitles on the Apple Neural Engine.

WhisperSub is a native macOS application and headless CLI that transcribes videos locally using OpenAI Whisper on Apple Silicon, formats clean .srt subtitle cues, and burns crisp captions directly into .mp4 video via hardware-accelerated AVFoundation and CoreImage.

End-to-End Pipeline

Four Steps from Raw Footage to Subtitled MP4

Every stage runs natively on your Mac without uploading confidential video or audio to external servers.

STEP 01 ⌘O

Drop & Inspect Video

Drag and drop .mp4, .mov, .m4v, .avi, .webm, or .mkv files. WhisperSub inspects resolution, frame rate, and duration while initializing live synchronized caption playback.

AVAsset • AVPlayerView
STEP 02 ⌘K

Optional Video Clipping

Set flexible start and end timestamps (HH:MM:SS.mmm, MM:SS, or directly from the current playhead) to trim the video before transcription so zero compute is wasted.

AVAssetExportSession • Fast Trim
STEP 03 ⌘T / ⌘S

Transcribe & Save .SRT

Extracts 16 kHz mono WAV audio, auto-detects spoken language, strips Whisper special tokens, splits long captions at 88 characters, and formats standard .srt cues.

WhisperKit CoreML • VAD Chunking
STEP 04 ⌘B

Burn .SRT into Video

Composites high-contrast, rounded subtitle overlays frame-by-frame onto the video track with real-time progress reporting and one-click Finder reveal.

AVVideoComposition • CoreImage

open WhisperSub.app

cd WhisperSub && ./build_app.sh && open WhisperSub.app
